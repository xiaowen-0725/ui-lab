# Brand reference attribution

The DESIGN.md documents in this directory are vendored from
[VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md)
at commit `664b3e78fd1a298ba11973822da988483256d4b4`.

The documents are distributed under the MIT License; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
They are community analyses extracted from publicly visible websites and are
not official brand guidelines.

The locally vendored first-viewport captures are stored for the inspiration
index with explicit project authorization. Their source URL, final URL,
capture date, dimensions, status, and asset kind are recorded in
`content/inspiration/screenshots-manifest.json`; clicking a preview returns to
the official website.
